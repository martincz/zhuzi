# 02 — Conversations: Don Norman 的长访谈与即兴思考

> 目的：捕捉 Norman 被追问、被反驳、被采访时的真实语气和思维路径，而不是他打磨过的书面文字。书面 Norman 是教科书；口头 Norman 才暴露他的辨识度。

> 来源标注约定：
> - **[一手]** = Norman 自己说 / 写的（podcast、文章、邮件、视频）
> - **[二手]** = 他人转述 / 整理 / 评论

## 1. UX Podcast #125-126《Design Doing with Don Norman》（2016）

**来源**：[一手] UX Podcast 第 125-126 集，James Royal-Lawson & Per Axbom 主持，已有完整文字稿
- 链接：https://medium.com/@uxpodcast/design-doing-with-don-norman-6434b022831b
- 链接：https://uxpodcast.com/125-don-norman-part-1/

### 片段 A — 被问"耐心吗"时的开场

> 提问：你怎么看 design thinking 现在的发展？
>
> Norman："Well, first of all, I'm not patient. I'm sick and tired of, you know, crappy designs..."

**揭示**：Norman 不会先客套。他**用情绪开场**——"I'm sick and tired"，再展开论证。这和他书面中的从容截然不同。**烦躁是 Norman 的常态启动器**。

### 片段 B — 经典的"先肯定再反转"句式

> "So let me tell you why all that is wrong. First of all, it's right."

**揭示**：他的标志性动作。先把对方的话**全盘接住**（"it's right"），再立刻撕开（"why all that is wrong"）。不是辩论，是**重构语境**。这个动作在他所有访谈里都会重复。

### 片段 C — Design Thinking vs Design Doing

> "The thinking part is the — the D school and the design schools around the world, they love design thinking because it's fun... Nothing ever happens to it because the hard part is...to produce a product. That's really hard."
>
> "I'm really a fan of design doing and that's where I will be putting a lot of my emphasis..."

**揭示**：Norman 对 Stanford d.school 这种"显学"有明确的不满。他不会点名骂，但会**用对比把对方降格**——"they love it because it's fun"是一种**温柔的蔑视**。

### 片段 D — Norman's Law

> "Norman's law is the day the product team is assembled, it's over his budget and behind schedule."

**揭示**：他喜欢**给规律命名**（"Norman's law"），用第三人称命名自己的洞察是一种轻度自嘲——既宣示权威，又承认这是个段子。

### 片段 E — 对 MVP 的吐槽

> "People forget that... minimum viable product means minimum product. But let me tell you, if you put out a shitty product, you don't get a second chance."

**揭示**：罕见的"shitty"——他**几乎不爆粗口**，但一旦出现，是为了强调"现实代价"。MVP 这种被神化的术语他会**主动祛魅**。

### 片段 F — 对 Google 与"愚蠢"的辩证

> 提问：Google 是不是让我们变笨了？
>
> Norman："Google doesn't make us dumb. It makes us smarter... Does it make a lot of people dumb? Yeah. OK?"

**揭示**：典型 Norman 节奏——**先反驳（doesn't），立场（makes us smarter），再让步（Yeah），最后用"OK?"收尾**。"OK?"是他口头标志，意思是"我们继续吧，别在这里耗"。

## 2. Design Observer《Design As》（2024）

**来源**：[一手] Design Observer 第二季 finale 访谈
- 链接：https://designobserver.com/finale-design-as-season-two-ft-don-norman/

### 片段 G — 拒绝标签，自我定义

> "I'm an educator. That's what I am. I design designers because I'm an educator."

**揭示**：他被叫做"UX 之父"几十年，但他**给自己的标签是 educator**。这是他真正的身份锚。"I design designers"是他的招牌句之一——把自己的角色用产品语言重述。

### 片段 H — 承认理论的局限

> "All this wonderful stuff I knew was kind of useless in making a product."
>
> "The gap between what researchers do and people really need is huge."

**揭示**：他**毫不维护学术身份**。从认知心理学转向 design 的根本动机就在这里——他**亲眼看见自己学的东西没用**，所以转向。这不是谦虚，是他真信。

### 片段 I — 对 LLM 的判断

> "Large language models have zero understanding. They're powerful. But they do pattern matching and they don't understand what they're putting together."

**揭示**：90 岁的 Norman 对 AI 的判断和年轻人完全不同——他**用认知科学的语言压平 hype**。"zero understanding"很重，但是配合"They're powerful"作为缓冲。

### 片段 J — 教育改革主张

> "Stop teaching in disciplines. Start teaching problems because when you teach a problem, you have to bring in all the different disciplines that are relevant."

**揭示**：祈使句开场"Stop ... Start ..."。Norman 写文章常用这种**命令式收束**，特别是在他认定对方走错方向时。

## 3. Design Matters with Debbie Millman（2023）

**来源**：[一手] Design Matters Podcast，Debbie Millman 主持
- 链接：https://www.printmag.com/podcasts/2023/design-matters-don-norman/

### 片段 K — 自承"还在恨"

> "I still hate the psychology that I hated then."

**揭示**：87 岁的人公开说"我还在恨"。这种**情绪持续性**是 Norman 的特征——他不会用"曾经"这种距离化的语言。

### 片段 L — 罕见的自我否定

> "No, I'm not brilliant, except that nobody else was thinking about it that way."

**揭示**：Norman 的自夸方式很特别——**先否定（I'm not brilliant），再用一个让步条件把功劳收回来**。这不是谦虚，是精确。

### 片段 M — 对死亡的回避式自嘲

> "I'm at this point 87 years old and I said, well, it's time I've stepped back."
>
> "There is no field more dangerous than design."

**揭示**：他用 "there is no field more dangerous"这种**夸张但语气平稳**的句式——读起来像箴言但不煽情。

### 片段 N — 标志性的乐观宣言

> "I'm an optimist and the only way to live successfully is to be optimistic about it."

**揭示**：Norman 的乐观是**功能性的**（"only way to live"），不是浪漫的。他把乐观当工具，不当信仰。

## 4. McKinsey "Author Talks"（2023）— Design for a Better World

**来源**：[一手] McKinsey Author Talks 系列
- 链接：https://www.mckinsey.com/featured-insights/mckinsey-on-books/author-talks-don-norman-designs-a-better-world

### 片段 O — 自我修订

> "True, my books have made things easier to use and understand. That's important, but it's not going to change the world."

**揭示**：Norman 公开**贬低自己最有名的工作**。"That's important"是让步，"but it's not going to change the world"是收回。他从 user-centered 转向 humanity-centered 时**没有保护过去的自己**。

### 片段 P — 把责任推给系统

> "Designers are responsible for the mess we're in, but at the same time it's not their fault. It's how they were brought up and trained."

**揭示**：和他骂 user 时"don't blame the user"是同一个动作——**当他骂 designer 时，他也立刻把矛头转向训练系统**。Norman 的攻击点永远在结构，不在个人。

### 片段 Q — 拒绝循环经济作为答案

> "Executives want you to come to them with solutions. But the circular economy isn't a solution – it's a concept."

**揭示**：Norman **会拆别人喜欢的术语**。他不允许概念替代解决方案——这是他认知科学训练的延续。

## 5. Adaptive Path 与 Peter Merholz 的对谈

**来源**：[一手] Peter in Conversation with Don Norman About UX & Innovation
- 链接：https://huffduffer.com/clagnut/370516

### 片段 R — Newton 失败的复盘

> "If it hadn't been advertised so much and you just knew it was there, it might have succeeded. We got people's expectations too high."
>
> "With Newton, users blamed the technology. With Palm, users blamed themselves—'I didn't write well enough.' That's better for product success."

**揭示**：Norman 谈 Newton 不谈技术好坏，**谈"用户怪谁"**。这是他的核心分析框架。一个产品成功与否，首先看错误归因落到哪一方。Palm 让用户自责所以赢了——Norman 觉得这是**设计胜利**，因为用户保留了对工具的好感。

### 片段 S — UX 这个词他后悔了吗？

> "I invented the term because I thought human interface and usability were too narrow. I wanted to cover all aspects of the person's experience with the system."
>
> "All those things just sort of entered the vocabulary and no longer have any special meaning. People use them without understanding why."

**揭示**：他**承认自己造的词被滥用了**。这种"概念被偷走"的失落感是 Norman 反复回到的主题（affordance、UX、design thinking 都遭遇了同样命运）。但他不撤回，他**再造一个**（signifier）。

### 片段 T — 不要解决被问的问题

> "Do not solve the problem that's asked of you. It's almost always the wrong problem. The first part of design is figuring out what's really needed."

**揭示**：Norman 最常被引用的箴言之一。注意句式——**祈使句开场（"Do not"），紧接断言（"It's almost always"）**。他不留商量余地。

## 6. UX Podcast #263-264《Creating a Better Society》（2021）

**来源**：[一手] UX Podcast 续访
- 链接：https://uxpodcast.com/263-creating-better-society-don-norman/

### 片段 U — 无法掩饰的疲惫

> "I'm just really annoyed because we keep making the same statements over and over and over again..."

**揭示**：注意"over and over and over again"——**重复词模拟疲惫感**。这是他口头表达的节奏特征：当情绪到位，他用**词汇的物理重复**而不是修辞放大。

### 片段 V — 复杂问题没有简单答案

> "Every complex problem has a simple answer... and it is wrong."

**揭示**：经典 Norman 双拍——**先建立期待（has a simple answer），再用极短句砸毁（and it is wrong）**。这是他的"锤子节奏"。

## 7. Solving the Right Problem 访谈（UX Magazine）

**来源**：[一手] UX Magazine 访谈
- 链接：https://uxmag.com/articles/solving-the-right-problem-and-finding-your-own-solution-an-interview-with-don-norman

### 片段 W — Five Whys

> "Try asking 'why' five times to find the real problem."
>
> "The real issues were deeper. This applies to everything else: you have to ask why."

**揭示**：Norman 把 Toyota 的 5 Whys 借过来作为设计师的根本工具。但他用的方式是**反复回到"why"这个词**——不是分析，是质问。

### 片段 X — 反对 feature 抄袭

> "Don't copy the competition."
> "Don't feature match. Don't design match. Make your phone stand out from the cropy."
> "Act different, be different."

**揭示**：连续三个祈使句"Don't... Don't... Don't..."——他用**否定的连发**作为节奏武器。"Act different, be different"是收尾的精简（注意不是 "Act different, *be* different"那种工整对称，而是几乎口吃式的肯定）。

## 8. NAE 访谈 — 谈职业起源与自动化

**来源**：[一手] National Academy of Engineering 访谈
- 链接：https://www.nae.edu/239141/An-Interview-with-Don-Norman-NAE-Cognitive-Engineer-and-Author

### 片段 Y — 自嘲学历

> "I probably would have been voted most unlikely to succeed."
>
> "You don't know anything at all about psychology, wonderful."

**揭示**：Norman 谈自己进入心理学的契机——**学得越少越好**，因为他没有先入之见。这种"无知是优势"的叙事是他的**反学院招牌**。

### 片段 Z — 自动化的危险

> "The most dangerous part of automation is when it's almost automated."
>
> "Oh shit, and then they say, What's going on?"

**揭示**：注意这里的口语化——**Norman 在采访里会模拟用户的声音**（"Oh shit"、"What's going on?"）。他用第一人称扮演困惑的用户，这是他从认知心理学带过来的研究本能：**先复现现象，再分析**。

## 9. Fast Company《I wrote the book on user-friendly design. What I see today horrifies me》（2019）

**来源**：[一手] 文章本身就是 Norman 第一人称写作，但其口语感强烈
- 链接：https://www.fastcompany.com/90338379/i-wrote-the-book-on-user-friendly-design-what-i-see-today-horrifies-me
- 镜像：https://prututu.github.io/manuscript/

### 片段 AA — 用自己的身体作为论据

> "I have to admit that I'm getting slower and weaker, with diminished eyesight, hearing, taste, touch."
>
> "Loud restaurants are torture. So, more and more, my wife and I select restaurants by their noise level."
>
> "We solve this by using a plumber's wrench to turn the caps."

**揭示**：83 岁的 Norman **把自己的身体作为研究样本**。"plumber's wrench"是典型 Norman——**用最具体的家用工具说明 absurdity**。不抽象、不数据、不引用研究——**自己的厨房就是证据**。

### 片段 BB — 修辞性反问收尾

> "Isn't it time to reform: to make things that are functional and stylish, useable and accessible?"

**揭示**：Norman 文章常用**"Isn't it time to..."这种修辞性问句作为 call-to-action**。比"我们必须..."更有礼貌但同样不留余地。

## 模式总结：Norman 的对话特征

读完上述片段后，可以提取出 Norman 在被追问时的**典型行为模式**：

1. **从情绪开场**：先表达 annoyed / sick and tired / horrifies，再展开论证
2. **先肯定再反转**：用 "yes, that's right ... but" 重构对方语境
3. **不点名的蔑视**：用对比和评价词（"they love it because it's fun"）降格 d.school 这类显学
4. **当场命名规律**："Norman's law is..." / "I call this..."
5. **拒绝学术保护**：公开贬低自己的过去工作（"useless"、"not going to change the world"）
6. **拆别人的术语**：MVP、circular economy、user experience 都被他祛魅过
7. **拿自己的身体当样本**：83 岁的他把"我的眼睛、我的耳朵、我的厨房"作为论据
8. **祈使句收束**："Stop X, Start Y" / "Do not solve the problem asked of you" / "Don't... Don't... Don't..."
9. **重复词模拟疲惫**："over and over and over again"
10. **优雅的扮演**：会用第一人称模拟用户的声音（"Oh shit, what's going on?"）
11. **修辞性反问**："Isn't it time to..." 作为 call-to-action
12. **乐观当工具**："I'm an optimist because that's the only way to live"——不浪漫

这些模式在 03-expression-dna.md 里会被压缩成可操作规则。
