## 1. 即兴套路：被追问时的转移 / 反问 / 拖延

### 1.1 "Many people are saying / A lot of people say"（来源不可追溯的权威化）

- **Lex Fridman #442 (2024-09-03)** [A]
  原文："A lot of people think the reason I didn't get hit is God was protecting me because he wanted this to happen."
  → 把个人命运包装成集体共识。

- **60 Minutes (2015-09-27, Scott Pelley)** [A]
  被问 ISIS 战略：
  > Pelley: "How?"
  > Trump: "There is a method of defeating them quickly and effectively and having total victory. Many people agree with me."
  → 拒绝披露细节时用 "many people agree" 兜底。

- **Joe Rogan #2219 (2024-10-25)** [A]
  原文："Everybody, Elon and everybody, Everybody says I'm the most honest human being that God ever created."
  （把"诚实"建立在"所有人的共识"而非可验证事实上）

### 1.2 "Nobody knows more about X than me"（资格抢夺）

公开 C-SPAN / Factbase 统计其至少 26 次不同场合使用，对象包括：ISIS、taxes、trade、drones、construction、campaign finance、debt、courts、Cory Booker、visas、renewable energy、technology、infrastructure、politicians、borders、banking、the H-1B、Facebook。

代表样本：
- **Hugh Hewitt 2015-09-03** [B]："Nobody knows banking better than I do."
- **CNBC 2016-05-05** [B]："Nobody knows more about debt. I'm like the king."
- **CPAC 2024-02-24**（非本次范围，仅补强一致性）："Nobody knows more about drones than me."

> 功能：抢在记者追问技术细节前关闭讨论；一旦被挑错，就切换到 "well, nobody really knows…" 的版本（见 1.4）。

### 1.3 "We'll see what happens" / "You'll find out"（延迟承诺的母 pattern）

- **CNN Jake Tapper 2016-07-24** [B]：关于是否接受选举结果 → "We'll see. We'll see what happens."
- **Lex Fridman #442 (2024-09-03)** [A]：关于 Ukraine 和平方案 → "I have a very exact plan on how to stop it, but if I tell you exactly, it's not going to work… you'll see."
- **Joe Rogan #2219** [A]：关于是否会再选 2028（一次玩笑）→ "We'll see what happens."
- **60 Minutes 2018-10-14 (Lesley Stahl)** [A]：关于 Mattis 是否留任 → "Well, we'll see. We'll see."

> 功能：保留可选项（optionality），拒绝被 quote 钉死；符合他在 Art of the Deal 里自述的 "hold on to as much flexibility as possible"。

### 1.4 反问 / 踢皮球

- **Lesley Stahl, 60 Minutes 2020-10-18** [A]（剪辑 + rev.com）：
  > Stahl: "But why weren't you prepared with testing?"
  > Trump: "Why weren't you? Why wasn't Obama prepared for the swine flu? Why wasn't Biden prepared for anything?"
- **Carroll Deposition 2022-10-19** [A]：
  > Q: "You said you were falsely accused?"
  > Trump: "Were you falsely accused? You look like you could have been falsely accused also."
- **E. Jean Carroll Deposition 2022-10-19** [A]：
  > Q: "Do you stand by your comments about the Access Hollywood tape being locker room talk?"
  > Trump: "Well, historically that's true with stars."
  → 把个人行为抽象成"历史规律"，避免被钉在具体语句上。

### 1.5 "With all due respect…" / "I have to be honest"（反向信号）

每次出现 "with all due respect" 之后几乎必是攻击；每次 "to be honest" 之后多半是夸大或未验证陈述。
- **Charlie Rose 1992** [B]："With all due respect to Charlie Rose, you wouldn't understand the real estate market the way I do." （Rose 的节目当时还在早期，Trump 用此句后紧跟自抬）
- **Lex Fridman #442** [A]："I hate to say it, but…" 后接针对 Harris 的嘲讽。

### 1.6 "I don't know him / I don't know her"（断亲切割）

当某关联者陷入麻烦时的标准距离化：
- **Lev Parnas（2019-10）**：记者问是否认识 → "I don't know him."（后照片证实多次合影）
- **Jeffrey Epstein（2019-07, 白宫问答）**："I was not a fan of his… I had a falling out with him a long time ago." ——注意结构：先否认，再回忆一个曾经的关系，以便保留解释空间。
- **Gary Cohn / Bannon 离任后同型套路**。

### 1.7 "That's a great question / good question"（抢占节奏）

常见在他没想好怎么答的时候：
- **Theo Von 2024-08-08** [B]：被问兄长 Freddy 去世 → "That's a great question, very personal. Very personal question."
- **Lex Fridman #442** [A]：被问 "What do you fear most?" → "That's a great question."（然后岔到 assassination）

### 1.8 拉长时间 / 重启叙事

"So…" 开头 + 回到自我起源故事：
- **O'Brien Deposition 2007** [A]：被问身家 → 从 "My father, as you know, was a great developer in Brooklyn and Queens…" 开始。
- **James Deposition 2022-08-10** [A]：开场长独白，虽然之后 440+ 次第五修正案，但第一轮仍坚持先定义"我们这个伟大的国家"。

## 2. 即兴类比库（building / deal / war / sports / money）

### 2.1 建筑 / 工程作为思维母题

- **Playboy 1990** [B]：论苏联 → "Their system is a disaster. What will be won is *freedom*. If they don't want freedom, what do they want? They want to be *rebuilt*."（把制度改革类比为拆旧楼盖新楼）
- **Charlie Rose 1992** [B]：论 NYC 治理 → "You have to treat the city like a building. You've got to have a strong foundation, you've got to have great management."
- **Joe Rogan #2219** [A]：论 federal government → "It's like a building that hasn't been maintained in 40 years. The pipes are shot."

### 2.2 Deal / Negotiation 作母型

- **Larry King 1987** [B]：论美日贸易 → "It's not a deal, it's a giveaway. Japan laughs at us."
- **Playboy 1990** [B]：论父亲 → "Fred Trump is the greatest man I've ever known. He understood one thing—the deal."
- **Lex Fridman #442** [A]：论 Putin → "I know him very well. It's a deal. Everything's a deal."
- **All-In Podcast 2024-06-20** [B]：论 crypto → "Bitcoin… it's a great thing. It's a form of currency. We want to be on the side of the deal, not against it."

### 2.3 War / 军事（多用于 rhetorical escalation 而非 literal）

- **Stern 1990s 系列**：性生活 = "my personal Vietnam" (1998-02 回忆，多次重复)。
- **Oprah 1988** [B]：论贸易 → "We are a debtor nation. We have to tax, we have to tariff, we have to protect this country."（把经济政策军事化为"保护"）
- **Joe Rogan #2219** [A]：论 tariffs → "Tariffs are the most beautiful word in the dictionary… It's like we're the bank, and they have to pay to come in."

### 2.4 Sports（尤其棒球、拳击、高尔夫）

- **Playboy 1990** [B]：论竞争 → "I love to have enemies. I fight my enemies. I like beating my enemies to the ground."（直接拳击语汇）
- **Stern 1995** [C]：评价对手 → "I'm a closer. I'm like Mariano Rivera." （棒球救援投手类比）
- **Lex Fridman #442** [A]：论 golf → "Golf is a great equalizer. You find out who people really are on a golf course."（高尔夫作为识人装置）

### 2.5 Money / 估值作计分板

- **O'Brien Deposition 2007** [A]：一段经典自我估值披露 —
  > Q: "How do you determine what your assets are worth?"
  > Trump: "My net worth fluctuates, and it goes up and down with markets and with attitudes and with feelings, even my own feelings."
  → 这是他难得当庭承认"估值取决于心情"的 smoking gun 段落。

- **Playboy 1990** [B]：论"什么时候才算够钱" → "I don't do it for the money. I've got enough, much more than I'll ever need. I do it to do it."

### 2.6 动物类比

- **Stern 1993** [C]：谈前女友 → "She was a great piece of ass." + "He's a lightweight"、"he's like a dog" 式将男性对手贬低为动物。
- **Lex Fridman #442** [A]：论媒体 → "They're like sharks. They smell blood."

## 4. 拒绝回答模式（silence / deflect / attack）

### 4.1 James Deposition 2022-08-10（完整模板）[A]

开场长独白后（约 5 分钟），然后：
- 问 1：关于 Mazars 财报 → "I decline to answer…"
- 问 2：关于 Trump Tower 估值 → "I decline to answer…"
- 问 3：关于 40 Wall Street → "I decline to answer…"

总计 **440+** 次几乎逐字重复的援引。这是 Trump 在**法律约束最强场合**的默认策略：**完全消音**。
（与 O'Brien 2007 和 Trump U 2015 形成鲜明对比——那两次没有刑事暴露，他**滔滔不绝**。）

### 4.2 Carroll Deposition 2022-10-19 的"攻击代替沉默"[A, rev.com]

- Q: "Do you know Ms. Carroll?"
- Trump: "I don't know her. I never met her. I wouldn't want to meet her… She's not my type. Physically, she's not my type."
  → **反击代替沉默**：当法律暴露"中等"时，用贬损对方外貌反击。
- 被展示照片问是否为 Carroll → Trump 把照片中的 Carroll 认成了 **Marla Maples**（他的前妻）。
  > "That's Marla. That's my wife."
  → 结构罕见：同时包含 recognition failure + 防御性否认。

### 4.3 Trump University Deposition 2015-12-10 的"修辞闪避"[A]

- Q: 关于是否亲自挑选 "handpicked" 讲师：
- Trump：一开始坚持 "I handpicked"，后被追问 → "Well, I may not have met every single one, but they came through a process that I approved of."
  → **降维闪避**：从 "I did it" 撤到 "I approved the process that did it"。
- Q: 关于广告语 "You will learn from me"：
- Trump: "It's advertising. Of course, it's advertising."
  → 公开承认广告语不是事实陈述。

### 4.4 O'Brien Deposition 2007-12-19/20 的"truthful hyperbole"确认[A]

Q: "Have you ever lied in public statements about your properties?"
A: "I try not to. I try… I will give myself the benefit of the doubt… A lot of times I say things without absolute proof."
Q: 关于 Trump Tower 其实是 58 楼却写 68 楼：
A: "I always said we went 68 floors…"
Q: 关于估值方法："Is the method by which you calculate your net worth… influenced by your own feelings?"
A: "Yes, even my own feelings, as to where the world is, where the world is going, and that can change rapidly from day to day…"
→ 这是**本研究最重要的一个一手引文**：他亲口把估值与"感觉"等同。

## 6. 承认错误的罕见样本（R-reflection 候选）

**数量上极少，所以都逐条记录**：

- **R1 — Playboy 1990** [B]：论第一次婚姻（Ivana） → "I think I was doing too much. I wasn't focused on Ivana. I think I was at fault, too."
  → 难得的共享过错陈述。

- **R2 — Larry King 1990（Atlantic City 危机后）** [B]："I was cocky in the 1980s. I got too sure of myself… I took my eye off the ball."
  → 事业危机后的反省，之后在 1992 Rose 里再次重复（"I wasn't paying attention to the details"）。

- **R3 — Charlie Rose 1992** [B]："The market did me in, but so did I. I borrowed too much."
  → 最接近"认错"的一次债务讨论。

- **R4 — 60 Minutes 2015-09 (Scott Pelley)** [A]：被问是否会道歉过某件事 → "Everybody makes mistakes. I mean, I've made many."（但拒绝具体化）

- **R5 — Stern 约 2008** [C]：被问"what was your biggest mistake" → "Probably I shouldn't have gotten into Atlantic City as big as I did."

- **R6 — Bob Woodward 2020-07-21 tape** [A]："I don't want to be known as a whiner." + 对 Covid "maybe we should have done more testing earlier." （极度迂回的承认）

- **R7 — Theo Von 2024-08-08** [B]：谈到兄长 Freddy 因酗酒早逝 →
  > "I wasn't helpful. I should have been… I should have been. I was so focused on my own career. I told him you got to stop… but I didn't really know how bad it was. I learned a lot from that."
  → **情绪上最柔软的一段公开长段**。Freddy 主题几乎是触发 Trump 共情模式的唯一稳定 trigger。

- **R8 — Lex Fridman #442 (2024-09-03)** [A]：被问是否怕死 → "I think about it now more than I used to. I didn't used to think about it at all."
  → 刺杀未遂后罕见的脆弱自述。

- **R9 — Trump University Deposition 2015** [A]：对 "handpicked" 的被迫修正（见 4.3）。这是**对抗性情境**下被迫的"退步"，严格说不是主动承认。

- **R10 — Playboy 1990** [B]："Being a little paranoid, I think, is a very good thing."
  → 把"多疑"命名为美德，同时也是罕见的性格自述（承认防御性性格）。

  → 在最亲和的节目环境下才出现。

## 8. Deposition 专题（法庭证词模式）

### 8.1 五次 deposition 的行为矩阵

| Deposition | 年份 | 刑事风险 | 主导模式 | 披露率 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| O'Brien libel | 2007 | 低（民事） | 长篇自我辩护 + 夸张修正 | **高**（意外披露了"estimates are feelings"） |
| Trump U | 2015 | 中（民事，大额） | 修辞闪避 / 语义降维 | 中 |
| Carroll | 2022-10 | 中（民事 + 名誉） | 反击 / 人身攻击 / 记错照片 | 中 |
| James / NY AG | 2022-08 | 高（刑事+民事边界） | **440+ 次第五修正案** | 极低 |
| Mazars / Vance 相关 | 2018-2022 | 高 | 律师主导 / 拒答 | 极低 |

### 8.2 Deposition 5 patterns（稳定规律）

1. **开场 overshare** — 不管后面要不要援引第五，开场都要把"国家 / 家族 / 成就"叙事铺一遍。
2. **记不清 / 记错对方** — Carroll 照片认成 Maples 是最戏剧性样本；O'Brien 中也多次 "I don't remember"，但同一天能记住 30 年前的业绩细节。**选择性记忆**。
3. **定义权争夺** — 把律师的具体问题拽到"整个体系是不公平的 / 这问题本身有问题"层面（Trump U 最明显）。
4. **被迫修正时用降维** — "I handpicked" → "I approved a process"；"I was worth 8.7 billion" → "it depends on feelings"。
5. **风险升高时 suddenly 哑** — James 庭上 440+ 次拒答；与 O'Brien 的多话形成 180° 对比。→ **法律暴露浓度与话量反比**。

## 10. 9 条矛盾 / Cognitive Tensions（供 skill anti-pattern 使用）

1. **自我估值 = 心情 (O'Brien 2007)** vs **"I know more about finance than anyone"** — 承认主观 vs 声称客观权威。
2. **"Mob takes the Fifth" (2016)** vs **本人援引 440+ 次 (James 2022)** — 语义一致性崩溃。
3. **Covid 私下"deadly" (Woodward 2020-02)** vs **公开 "like a flu" (2020-02-26)** — 主动承认信息双轨制。
4. **"I don't know Carroll"** vs **同一 deposition 照片中认成 Maples** — 否认与无意识识别同时存在。
5. **关税"最美丽的词"** vs **商人身份长期反对对自家产业征税** — 意识形态 vs 私人利益冲突，Trump 从未在访谈中正面解释。
6. **"I don't do it for the money"** (1990) vs **终身围绕地产估值和 Forbes 排名战斗** — 口头去物质化 / 行为超物质化。
7. **"I love to have enemies" (Playboy 1990)** vs **Theo Von 兄长片段的柔软** — 硬派人设 vs 家族创伤共情的稀缺窗口。
8. **强人崇拜（Putin/Xi/Kim）** vs **"我是谈判家"** — 谈判需要灵活性，但强人崇拜要求刚性；Trump 的实际行为是**对外刚 + 对强人软**。
9. **"Nobody knows more than me about X"** 同一天用在 13+ 个互不相关领域 (Hewitt, CNBC, rallies) — 资格声明的**通胀**使其对听者实际**信息量为零**。

## 13. 为 skill 蒸馏的结构化建议

1. **6 个心智模型雏形（待蒸馏）**
   - **Everything is a deal**（Ontology of negotiation）
   - **Perception = Reality**（"truthful hyperbole"）
   - **Optionality by ambiguity**（"we'll see what happens"）
   - **Enemy as fuel**（"I love to have enemies"）
   - **Brand as asset class**（estimates = feelings）
   - **Alpha signaling**（最高级 / believe me / nobody knows more）

2. **12 条 anti-pattern 识别器**（对用户"你是否在走 Trump 套路"的负向检测）
   - "Many people are saying"
   - "Nobody knows more than me"
   - "We'll see"
   - `[形容词] + [名字]` 人身标签
   - 反问代替回答
   - 估值 / 数据基于"感觉"
   - 事后降维（handpicked → approved）
   - 否认亲密关系前用"I had a falling out"
   - 选择性记忆（"I don't recall" 同日记住 30 年前细节）
   - 双轨叙事（私下版本 vs 公开版本）
   - 强人崇拜 + 盟友贬损同句
   - 最高级压舱（"the most beautiful word in the dictionary"）

3. **4 档表达强度**
   - D档：Stern / Theo Von 级（粗粝 + 罕见共情）
   - C档：Rogan / Larry King 级（标签 + 节奏三拍）
   - B档：Lex Fridman / Charlie Rose 级（类比 + 命运叙事）
   - A档：Deposition 级（法律约束下的极简否认或完全拒答）

4. **6 条诚实边界（不应包装）**
   - 这是谈判 / 品牌 / 媒体战 playbook，**不是道德哲学**
   - 估值"取决于感觉"不能作为用户投资建议
   - 长期立场的稳定部分（关税、NATO、核威胁）≠ 正确
   - 否认与事实共存的样本（Carroll 照片）应标注为**人物研究样本**而非可复制话术
   - 第五修正案场景不可当商业策略
   - 不要生成具体贬损真实人物的内容
